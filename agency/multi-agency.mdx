GoTravelXpress allows you to manage multiple transport agencies from a single license. This module is reserved for Premium and Enterprise plans and gives the administrator the ability to create, configure, and oversee several independent terminals while maintaining a consolidated view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Log in to GoTravelXpress at `index.php`, then navigate to **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your license">
    Make sure your license is **Premium** or **Enterprise**. Only these plans allow multi-agency mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new agency">
    In **Settings > Agencies**, click **Add agency**. Enter the name, city, currency, time zone, and upload the logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set dedicated routes">
    For each agency, configure its own routes. Lines, stations, and schedules are independent across agencies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign users">
    Create Operator, Baggage Handler, and Hostess roles by linking each user to a specific agency. They can only access their own agency's data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable optional sub-admins">
    Designate a sub-administrator per agency to manage day-to-day operations: assignments, routes, and staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review analytics">
    Filter reports by agency or view the consolidated dashboard to see all activity at a glance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Multiple sites" icon="building">
    Each agency has its own name, city, currency, time zone, and routes. You run several terminals without any data conflict.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Isolated roles" icon="shield-halved">
    Counter Operators, Baggage Handlers, and Hostesses are restricted to their own agency. They cannot see or modify data from other sites.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Admin consolidation" icon="chart-pie">
    The main administrator keeps a cross-agency view. Dashboards and reports can be filtered by agency or displayed globally.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Per-agency branding" icon="palette">
    Each agency can display its own logo in the interface. The Enterprise plan also supports full white-label.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
