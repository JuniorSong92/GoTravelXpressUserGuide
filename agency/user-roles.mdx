GoTravelXpress organizes its users around **7 clearly separated profiles**. Each profile only sees the modules it needs, which simplifies training, secures operations, and delivers a clean audit trail. This page details the concrete operations each profile performs, its workspace (web or mobile), and its permissions.

## Detailed role breakdown

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Admin / Owner">
    ### <Icon icon="key" iconType="solid" /> Administrator / Owner

    License holder. Full control over the platform.

    **Operations performed by the Admin:**

    - Manages the **intelligent planning** (calendar, trip assignment, conflict resolution).
    - Creates **Driver** accounts and assigns them to buses.
    - Adds **Operators**, **Baggage Handlers**, **Hostesses** and manages their permissions.
    - Creates and manages the **bus fleet** (registration, capacity, seat plan).
    - Consults the **analytics dashboard** (revenue, load factor, per-agency KPIs).
    - Manages the **subscription** and license activation (Free Trial / Pro Plan).
    - Adds and updates the **FAQ** and **facilities** shown to customers.
    - Creates and manages **agencies** (multi-agency setup).
    - Configures **payment methods** (Mobile Money, cards, cash) and pricing.
    - Sets ticketing rules (cancellation, refund policy, seat lock time).
    - Reviews **security logs** and audit trails.
    - Exports reports (CSV, PDF).

    **Workspace:** Global dashboard with real-time KPIs, alerts, recent activity, per-agency charts, and complete settings menu.

    <Note>
      Only the Admin account sees **Settings > License**. License activation is never delegated.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Operator">
    ### <Icon icon="ticket" iconType="solid" /> Agency Operator

    Counter role. Handles sales, routes, and customer finances.

    **Operations performed by the Operator:**

    - **Books reservations** for customers (counter, phone, or on behalf).
    - Creates and edits **routes** (departure, arrival, intermediate stops).
    - Creates **drop-off and pick-up points** on each route.
    - Issues QR-coded tickets and sends them by SMS, email, or WhatsApp.
    - Collects payments (**Mobile Money, card, cash**).
    - Manages **finance operations and refunds** for own agency (cancellations, partial refunds).
    - Manages **parcel shipments** on behalf of the customer.
    - Generates invoices and receipts.
    - Reschedules or cancels a ticket according to Admin policy.
    - Views own sales history and cash desk balance.

    **Workspace:** Today's trips, real-time seat map, open cash register, sales history, parcel counter.

    <Tip>
      The Operator does not activate the license, does not create staff, and cannot change global pricing rules. Those actions stay with the Administrator.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Baggage Handler">
    ### <Icon icon="suitcase-rolling" iconType="solid" /> Baggage Handler

    In charge of bags and parcels for every trip.

    **Operations performed by the Baggage Handler:**

    - **Manages baggage**: registers each bag (weight, description, owner) with the passenger's QR ticket.
    - **Manages parcels**: attaches parcels to a route, records the recipient, prints the label.
    - Generates and prints barcode or QR labels for each item.
    - Marks each item as **Loaded**, **Delivered**, or **Unclaimed**.
    - Searches a bag or parcel by tag, ticket number, or recipient.
    - Performs **clock-in and clock-out** according to their planning (geolocated QR).
    - Reports lost or damaged items to the Administrator.

    **Workspace:** Trips in check-in, bag/parcel counter per trip, integrated scanner, personal planning.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hostess / Crew Chief">
    ### <Icon icon="plane-departure" iconType="solid" /> Hostess / Crew Chief

    On-board role. Controls tickets and clocks in.

    **Operations performed by the Hostess:**

    - **Scans and validates QR tickets** at boarding.
    - Marks each passenger as **Boarded**, **No-show**, or **Late**.
    - Performs **clock-in and clock-out** according to their planning (geolocated QR).
    - Views the passenger list of the assigned trip in real time.
    - Reports an on-board incident (alert forwarded to the Admin).
    - Confirms trip closure on arrival.
    - Verifies seat occupancy during the trip.

    **Workspace:** Current trip, seat map with statuses, scanner module, alert button, personal planning.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Driver">
    ### <Icon icon="steering-wheel" iconType="solid" /> Driver

    Bus operator. Mobile app only.

    **Operations performed by the Driver:**

    - Signs in to the mobile app (credentials or Face Login).
    - Views assigned trips of the day.
    - Scans the **start-of-service QR** (geolocated clock-in).
    - Tracks boarded passengers, stops, and conflict alerts in real time.
    - Scans the **end-of-service QR** on arrival (geolocated clock-out).
    - Receives planning conflict alerts.
    - Reports a mechanical or road incident from a dedicated form.
    - Views own trip history.

    **Workspace:** Mobile app exclusively, no web accounting access.

    <Warning>
      The Driver profile has **no web dashboard**. All activity runs through the mobile app with mandatory GPS authorization.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Client">
    ### <Icon icon="user" iconType="solid" /> Client (Passenger)

    End user of the service. Public-facing.

    **Operations performed by the Client:**

    - Searches a route (departure, arrival, date).
    - Picks a seat on the interactive plan.
    - Books one or several seats in a single order.
    - Pays online (**Mobile Money, Visa/Mastercard**).
    - Receives the QR ticket by email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
    - Tracks their **baggage or parcel** in real time (Registered → Loaded → Delivered).
    - Consults own reservation and receipt history.
    - Cancels or reschedules a ticket according to the agency policy.
    - Contacts support directly from the account.
    - Consults the **FAQ** and browses agency facilities.
    - Signs in with Face Login on mobile.

    **Workspace:** Personal account (web or mobile) with reservation history, active tickets, receipts, and parcel tracking.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Agent">
    ### <Icon icon="users" iconType="solid" /> Agent (versatile)

    Generic profile that bundles **Operator + Baggage Handler + Hostess** for versatile field employees.

    **How it works:**

    In the mobile app, "Agency Agent" is a hub that switches between three activities according to the permissions the Admin grants:

    - **Operator mode**: mobile counter sale, encashment, parcel management.
    - **Baggage Handler mode**: baggage/parcel registration, label printing, clock-in.
    - **Hostess mode**: boarding scan, passenger validation, clock-in.

    The tabs shown to the Agent depend directly on the mix of permissions assigned in their user record.

    <Tip>
      Use the Agent profile in small agencies where the same person handles several stages of the journey. In larger agencies, prefer specialized roles for clean traceability.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create a user account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open user management">
    In the Admin dashboard, go to **Users > New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the identity">
    Full name, business email, phone, and home agency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the role">
    Select **Admin**, **Operator**, **Baggage Handler**, **Hostess**, **Driver**, or **Agent (versatile)**. Permissions apply automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial password">
    The account receives an activation email with a temporary password to change on first sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Principle of least privilege" icon="lock">
    Only assign the Administrator role to people who actually need to manage the platform and license.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One account per person" icon="user">
    Avoid shared accounts. Per-user traceability is essential for incident or financial audits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Revoke quickly" icon="user-slash">
    Immediately disable an account when an employee leaves. Do not delete their history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Use Face Login" icon="camera">
    For exposed posts (counter, boarding), enable Face Login on top of the password.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
