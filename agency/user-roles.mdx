GoTravelXpress runs on a strict four-role model. Each account only accesses the modules it needs, which simplifies training, secures operations, and cleanly separates responsibilities.

## Overview

| Role | Scope | License access | Finance access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator / Manager | Whole platform and all agencies | Yes — activates the key | Full |
| Agency Operator | Sales, reservations, payments | No | Own agency cash desk |
| Baggage Handler | Baggage, parcels, labels | No | None |
| Hostess / Crew Chief | Boarding, crew clock-in | No | None |

## Administrator / Manager

The license-owner role. It configures the whole platform.

**Key permissions:**

- Activate and renew the license key.
- Create, edit, or delete agencies.
- Create Operator, Baggage Handler, and Hostess accounts and assign their role.
- Manage the fleet (buses), routes, and pricing tables.
- Plan trips and assign driver + hostess.
- Open and close sales.
- View all statistics and export reports.
- Configure payment methods (Mobile Money, cards, cash).
- Access security logs and general settings.

**Dashboard:** global view with KPIs (trips, tickets, revenue, load factor), alerts, recent activity, and per-agency charts.

## Agency Operator

The counter role. Opens sales and collects payments from customers.

**Key permissions:**

- View open trips for their agency.
- Reserve a seat for a customer (at the counter or by phone).
- Issue a ticket with QR code.
- Collect Mobile Money, card, or cash payments.
- Generate an invoice or receipt.
- Cancel or reschedule a ticket per the policy set by the Admin.
- View sales history for their post.

**Dashboard:** today's trips, real-time seat map, open cash register, recent sales.

<Tip>
  An operator cannot change prices, create a route, or unlock a module. Those actions stay with the Administrator.
</Tip>

## Baggage Handler

The role in charge of bags and parcels at check-in and delivery.

**Key permissions:**

- Scan the passenger's QR ticket to link it to their baggage.
- Register each bag or parcel with weight, description, and recipient.
- Generate and print a label with barcode or QR.
- View the baggage list for a trip.
- Mark a bag as **Loaded**, **Delivered**, or **Unclaimed**.
- Search a bag by tag number or ticket.

**Dashboard:** trips in check-in, bag counter per trip, integrated scanner.

## Hostess / Crew Chief

The on-board role. Controls tickets and clocks in the crew.

**Key permissions:**

- Scan each QR ticket at boarding to validate access.
- Mark a passenger as **Boarded**, **No-show**, or **Late**.
- Clock in their own attendance with a geolocated QR.
- View the passenger list for the assigned trip.
- Report an on-board incident (alert button forwarded to the Admin).
- Confirm trip closure on arrival.

**Dashboard:** current trip, seat map with passenger status, scanner module, alert button.

## Create a user account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open user management">
    In the Admin dashboard, go to **Users > New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the identity">
    Full name, business email, phone, and home agency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the role">
    Select **Administrator**, **Agency Operator**, **Baggage Handler**, or **Hostess**. Permissions are applied automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial password">
    The account receives an activation email with a temporary password to change on first sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Principle of least privilege" icon="lock">
    Only assign the Administrator role to people who actually need to manage the platform and license.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One account per person" icon="user">
    Avoid shared accounts: per-user traceability is essential for incident or financial audits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Revoke quickly" icon="user-slash">
    Immediately disable an account when an employee leaves the agency, without deleting their history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Use Face Login" icon="camera">
    For exposed posts (counter, boarding), enable Face Login on top of the password.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
