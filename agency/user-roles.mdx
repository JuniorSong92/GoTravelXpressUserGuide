GoTravelXpress organizes its users around **7 clearly separated profiles**. Each profile only sees the modules it needs, which simplifies training, secures operations, and delivers a clean audit trail. This page explains what each profile can do, where it works (web or mobile), and which permissions it holds.

## Detailed role breakdown

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Admin / Owner">
    ### <Icon icon="key" iconType="solid" /> Administrator / Owner

    The license-owner role. Full control over the platform.

    **Key permissions:**

    - Activate and renew the license key.
    - Create, edit, or delete agencies.
    - Create Operator, Baggage Handler, Hostess, and Driver accounts.
    - Manage the fleet (buses), routes, and pricing tables.
    - Plan trips and assign driver + hostess.
    - Open and close sales.
    - Configure payment methods (Mobile Money, cards, cash).
    - Access all statistics and export reports.
    - Access security logs and general settings.

    **Workspace:** Global dashboard with KPIs (trips, tickets, revenue, load factor), alerts, recent activity, and per-agency charts.

    <Note>
      Only Admin accounts see **Settings > License**. License activation is never delegated.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Operator">
    ### <Icon icon="ticket" iconType="solid" /> Agency Operator

    The counter role. Opens sales and collects payments.

    **Key permissions:**

    - View open trips for their agency.
    - Reserve a seat for a customer (counter or phone).
    - Issue a QR-coded ticket.
    - Collect Mobile Money, card, or cash payments.
    - Generate an invoice or receipt.
    - Cancel or reschedule a ticket per Admin policy.
    - View their own sales history.

    **Workspace:** Today's trips, real-time seat map, open cash register, recent sales.

    <Tip>
      An Operator cannot change prices, create a route, or unlock a module. Those actions stay with the Administrator.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Baggage Handler">
    ### <Icon icon="suitcase-rolling" iconType="solid" /> Baggage Handler

    In charge of bags and parcels at check-in and delivery.

    **Key permissions:**

    - Scan the passenger's QR ticket to link it to their baggage.
    - Register each bag or parcel (weight, description, recipient).
    - Generate and print a barcode or QR label.
    - View the baggage list for a trip.
    - Mark a bag as **Loaded**, **Delivered**, or **Unclaimed**.
    - Search a bag by tag number or ticket.

    **Workspace:** Trips in check-in, bag counter per trip, integrated scanner.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hostess / Crew Chief">
    ### <Icon icon="plane-departure" iconType="solid" /> Hostess / Crew Chief

    The on-board role. Controls tickets and clocks in the crew.

    **Key permissions:**

    - Scan each QR ticket at boarding.
    - Mark a passenger as **Boarded**, **No-show**, or **Late**.
    - Clock in own attendance with geolocated QR.
    - View the passenger list of the assigned trip.
    - Report an on-board incident (alert to Admin).
    - Confirm trip closure on arrival.

    **Workspace:** Current trip, seat map with statuses, scanner module, alert button.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Driver">
    ### <Icon icon="steering-wheel" iconType="solid" /> Driver

    Bus operator. Mobile app only.

    **Key permissions:**

    - Sign in to the mobile app (credentials or Face Login).
    - View assigned trips of the day.
    - Scan the **start-of-service QR** (geolocated).
    - Track boarded passengers, stops, and conflict alerts.
    - Scan the **end-of-service QR** on arrival.
    - Receive planning conflict alerts.

    **Workspace:** Mobile app only. No web access to accounting.

    <Warning>
      The Driver profile has **no web dashboard**. All activity runs through the mobile app with mandatory GPS.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Client">
    ### <Icon icon="user" iconType="solid" /> Client (Passenger)

    The end user of the service. Public-facing.

    **Web access:**

    - Search a route.
    - Pick a seat on the interactive map.
    - Pay online (Mobile Money, card).
    - Receive the QR ticket by email/SMS/WhatsApp.

    **Mobile app access:**

    - Book directly from the phone.
    - QR ticket stored offline for scanning at boarding.
    - Real-time baggage/parcel tracking (Registered → Loaded → Delivered).
    - Face Login for quick sign-in.

    **Workspace:** Personal account with reservation history, active tickets, and receipts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Agent">
    ### <Icon icon="users" iconType="solid" /> Agent (polyvalent)

    A generic profile bundling **Operator + Baggage Handler + Hostess** for versatile field employees.

    **How it works:** In the mobile app, "Agency Agent" is a hub that switches between three activities according to the permissions the Admin grants:

    - Mobile counter sale (Operator mode)
    - Baggage check-in (Baggage Handler mode)
    - Boarding control (Hostess mode)

    The tabs displayed to the Agent depend directly on the mix of permissions assigned in their user record.

    <Tip>
      Use the Agent profile in small agencies where the same person handles several stages of the journey. In large agencies, prefer specialized roles (Operator, Baggage Handler, Hostess) for clean traceability.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create a user account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open user management">
    In the Admin dashboard, go to **Users > New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the identity">
    Full name, business email, phone, and home agency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the role">
    Select **Admin**, **Operator**, **Baggage Handler**, **Hostess**, **Driver**, or **Agent (polyvalent)**. Permissions apply automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial password">
    The account receives an activation email with a temporary password to change on first sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Principle of least privilege" icon="lock">
    Only assign the Administrator role to people who actually need to manage the platform and license.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One account per person" icon="user">
    Avoid shared accounts. Per-user traceability is essential for incident or financial audits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Revoke quickly" icon="user-slash">
    Immediately disable an account when an employee leaves. Do not delete their history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Use Face Login" icon="camera">
    For exposed posts (counter, boarding), enable Face Login on top of the password.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
