## Dashboard

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Dashboard).</Info>

The Dashboard is the first view when opening the Hostess app. It centralizes all operational information for the day so you can prepare your shift and prioritize actions.

### Today's View

**Today's View** shows a complete summary of the day's fleet: number of buses in service, expected passengers, and pending requests. You can quickly search for a passenger by name, phone number, or ticket reference. The view also displays a service checklist, imminent departures with countdown timers, operational alerts (delays, overbooking, gate changes), and the latest boarding actions completed.

### Active Trips

The **Active Trips** section details all trips scheduled for the day. Each row shows the bus, destination, departure time, current status (on time, delayed, cancelled), and an action button to jump directly to tickets or boarding control for that trip.

## Tickets

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Tickets).</Info>

The Tickets section gathers all reservations linked to the hostess's trips. It lets you review passengers, their boarding status, and intervene manually when needed.

### Reservation List

The **Reservation List** displays all tickets booked on the hostess's trips. Each ticket shows the passenger name, assigned seat, boarding status (pending, boarded, no-show), and consultation actions to view the full ticket details.

### Pending Trips (Total Pending Trip)

The **Pending Trips** section lists trips whose boarding has not yet started. Expanding a trip reveals the registered passenger list and the seat map. This view helps you anticipate passenger counts and prepare control before the bus arrives.

## Boarding Scan

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Boarding Scan).</Info>

Boarding Scan is the core of the hostess role. This feature lets you verify ticket authenticity and validate passenger access to the bus.

### Scan a Ticket

Two verification methods are available:

1. **QR code scan**: use the device camera to scan the QR code printed on the paper ticket or displayed on the passenger's phone. The app instantly validates the ticket and displays passenger information.
2. **Manual search**: enter the ticket number, phone number, or passenger name in the search field. Results appear in real time.

In both cases, press the **Verify Ticket** button to finalize the passenger boarding. The ticket status changes to "boarded" and the passenger may proceed onto the bus.

## Trip Report

<Info>
  Screenshots coming soon (Trip Report).
</Info>

After each trip, the hostess can review trip reports submitted by drivers or other agents. These reports serve as operational records and a basis for any disputes.

### Report by Trip

The **Report by Trip** section lists all submitted trip reports. Each report is linked to a bus and driver, with submission date and time. Opening a report reveals details: number of passengers carried, reported incidents, registered baggage, and driver observations.

## My Profile

<Info>
  Screenshots coming soon (My Profile).
</Info>

The Profile page centralizes the hostess's personal information and security settings.

### Hostess Profile

The **Hostess Profile** section displays personal information: employee ID, gender, date of birth, phone number, email address, postal address, ID number, and active/inactive status. Some fields can be updated directly from this page.

A dedicated block enables facial recognition enrollment. Follow the on-screen instructions (position your face toward the camera, turn your head as indicated) to register your biometrics for future boarding checks and quick authentications.

## Leave & Planning

<Info>
  Screenshots coming soon (Leave & Planning).
</Info>

The Leave & Planning section lets the hostess manage absences and review work schedules.

### Leave Request

The **Leave Request** form includes the following fields: start date, end date, leave type (annual leave, sick leave, exceptional leave), and detailed reason. Once submitted, the request appears in the tracking table with its status (pending, approved, rejected). You can review your full request history and manager comments.

### My Planning (Smart Planning)

The **My Planning** view displays your personal schedule as a calendar or list. Each shift shows the service title, period (date and hours), work location, and status (scheduled, in progress, completed, cancelled). **Check In** and **Check Out** buttons let you clock in and out in real time, ensuring automatic attendance tracking.
