## Dashboard

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Dashboard).</Info>

The Operator Dashboard provides a real-time overview of your agency's activity. You will find the following key indicators:

- **Number of buses**, **stations served**, **passengers carried** and **pending parcels**: these counters reflect your agency's immediate activity.
- **Gross and net earnings**: gross earnings represent total sales before deductions. Net earnings are calculated with or without the administrative commission deducted by the platform.
- **Total reservations**: this block distinguishes reservations made by your agency's agents from those made directly by you as the operator.
- **Pending withdrawal amount**: track at a glance the fund withdrawal requests that are being processed.

This dashboard enables you to make quick decisions and prioritize your actions every day.

## Parcels

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Parcels).</Info>

The **Parcels** section centralizes your agency's shipment management. You can view the history, add a new parcel or edit an existing shipment.

### Parcel History

The parcel list displays all shipments created by the operator. Each row shows the essential information:

- **Sender** and **recipient**
- **Bus** assigned to the transport
- **Parcel status** (delivered, in progress, pending)

Action icons let you **view the details** of a parcel or **change its status** directly from the list. Use these actions to keep your shipment tracking up to date and inform your customers.

### Add / Edit a Parcel

The parcel creation or editing form is organized in two parts. Scroll down to access all fields.

**Top section:**

- **Trip date**: select the bus departure date.
- **Sender / recipient information**: name, phone number and ID number.
- **Parcel nature**: description of the shipment contents.
- **Transport letter number**: reference of the transport document.
- **City and departure point** and **city and arrival point**: complete itinerary.
- **Price per kilometer**, **weight** and **distance**: data used to calculate the fare.

**Bottom section:**

- **Final price**: total amount of the shipment.
- **Payment method**: selected mode of payment.
- **Assigned bus**: vehicle that will carry the parcel.
- **Seller / admin commission**: share for the seller and commission deducted by the administration.
- **Parcel status**.
- **Transaction number**.
- **Wallet amount used**: if the customer pays via their electronic wallet.
- **Detailed description**: additional notes about the shipment.

Click the **Add Parcel** button to confirm the creation or modification of the parcel.

## Book Ticket

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Book Ticket).</Info>

Ticket sales take place in four successive steps. Follow each step to finalize a reservation as an operator.

### 1. Search for a Trip

Start by defining the search criteria:

- **Departure city**
- **Arrival city**
- **Travel date**
- **Bus type**

Launch the search. Available trips are displayed as cards. Each card shows the departure and arrival times, the ticket price and a **View Seats** button to proceed to the next step.

### 2. Choose Seats

An interactive bus layout appears, with the distinction between the **lower deck** and the **upper deck** when the vehicle has both. Click on available seats to select them. Seats that are already reserved or occupied are not clickable. Once your selection is made, proceed to the next step.

### 3. Confirm Pick-up and Drop-off Points

This step lets you activate the **pick-up point** and the **drop-off point** from those offered for the selected trip. Choose the stops that match the customer's needs, then continue to the final form.

### 4. Enter Passengers, Contact and Payment

The final form gathers travel and payment information. Scroll down to access all fields.

**Passenger information:**

For each selected seat, enter:

- **Name**
- **Age**
- **Gender**

**Contact details:**

- **Contact name**
- **Email**
- **Phone country code**
- **Phone number**

**Price summary:**

- **Total price**
- **Tax**
- **Total to pay**

At the bottom of the form, enter the **amount received from the customer**. The system automatically calculates the **change to give back**. Click the **Book Ticket** button to finalize the sale and generate the tickets.

## Trip Tracking

<Info>Screenshots coming soon (Trip Tracking).</Info>

The **Trip Tracking** section lets you view the history of trips managed by your agency, whether completed or cancelled.

### Completed Trips

This list groups finished trips. Each entry displays:

- **Bus name**
- **Number of passengers**
- **Customer rating** in stars
- **Comments** left by travelers

This information helps you evaluate service quality and identify areas for improvement.

### Cancelled Trips

This list shows trips that have been cancelled. For each cancellation you will find:

- **Rejection reason**: why the trip was cancelled
- **Amount concerned**: the sum linked to the cancellation, useful for financial tracking and any refunds

## Cash Register

<Info>
  Screenshots coming soon (Cash Register).
</Info>

**Cash Register** brings together all the tools for tracking and managing your agency's cash flow.

### Cash Register Dashboard

The daily overview shows:

- **Today's revenue**
- **COD to collect** (Cash on Delivery)
- **Pending refunds**
- **Today's expenses**

You will also find:

- **Monthly revenue and expenses**
- **Monthly net result**
- **Revenue vs expenses chart for the last 7 days**

**Action shortcuts** provide quick access to the following functions: new expense, new refund, collect COD, manage wallets.

<Tip>
  A red banner reminds you to open the cash register if no session is active. Open your cash session at the start of the day to correctly record all movements.
</Tip>

### Cash Journal

This section manages the **day's cash session**:

- **Opening** and **closing** the session
- **History of previous sessions**: opening date, closing date, discrepancy found and status
- **Latest recorded movements**: real-time tracking of cash inflows and outflows

### Cash Transactions

The exhaustive list of receipts and disbursements includes:

- **Movement type**
- **Reference**
- **Amount**
- **Payment method**
- **Status**

**Total collected and disbursed amounts** are displayed at the top of the page for a quick balance.

### Refunds

Track refund requests sorted by status:

- **Pending**
- **Approved**
- **Processed**
- **Rejected**

Each request can be viewed in detail to verify its origin and amount.

### Expenses

Record your agency's expenses with:

- **Expense category**
- **Amount**
- **Period**

A breakdown chart by category helps you visualize the structure of your costs.

### COD Payments

This section tracks parcels and tickets paid on delivery. Amounts are sorted into four states:

- **Pending**
- **Collected**
- **Deposited in cash register**
- **Cancelled**

### Wallets

The global wallets view shows:

- **Total balances in circulation**
- **Number of active customers**

You can view the **flow history** and **search for a specific customer** to display their balance and movements.
