## Dashboard

Once signed in, the Admin lands on the **Dashboard (Report Data)**, which shows a synthetic view of all GoTravelXpress activity through KPI cards.

<Frame caption="Admin Dashboard: banners, cities, buses, facilities, FAQ, payment gateways, dynamic pages, users, agents, operators, cumulative earnings and taxes received">
  ![GoTravelXpress Admin dashboard](/images/guide/admin/dashboard.png)
</Frame>

This is the home screen after signing in. It groups:

- **Content items**: banners, cities, buses, facilities, FAQ, dynamic pages, payment gateways.
- **Platform users**: Total Users, Total Agents, Total Operators.
- **Earnings**: Operator Earning (self-book / non self-book), Agent Earning, Admin Earning, Tax Received.

<Tip>
  The left sidebar gives access to every module: Dashboard, Analytics, Subscription, Agency, Planning, Hostess, Porters, Parcel Order, Banner, City, Drop/Pick Point, Bus Operator, Facility, FAQ, Cancel Policy, Reports.
</Tip>

## Agency management

The Admin is the only role allowed to create, edit and publish partner agencies.

### Add an agency

<Frame caption="Add Agency form: name, country, city, neighborhood, address, phone, email, status, license number, number of buses, description, creation year">
  ![Add an agency](/images/guide/admin/agency-add.png)
</Frame>

Fields to fill in:

- **Agency Name**, **Phone**, **Email**
- **Country**, **City Name**, **Neighborhood** (dropdowns)
- **Agency Status** (Publish / Unpublish)
- **Full address**, **Commercial license number**
- **Number of buses**, **Description**, **Creation year**

Click **Add Agency** to save.

### Agency list

<Frame caption="Agency Management: table of registered agencies with logo, name, country, city, contact and publish status">
  ![Agency list](/images/guide/admin/agency-list.png)
</Frame>

From this table, the Admin can:

- **Search** and **sort** agencies.
- Check the **status** (Active / Inactive, Publish / Unpublish).
- **Edit**, **enable/disable** or **delete** each agency.

## Intelligent planning

The **Smart Planning** module (premium) lets the Admin steer Hostess and Baggage Handler presence and auto-generate schedules.

<Frame caption="Smart Planning: KPIs (presence rate, present today, late, leave pending, peak hours, coverage) and schedule notifications">
  ![Smart Planning Admin](/images/guide/admin/planning-smart.png)
</Frame>

**Quick actions at the top of the page:**

- **Request Leave**: submit a leave request.
- **Team Rotation**: manage team rotations.
- **Auto Generate**: automatically generate an optimized schedule.

**KPIs displayed:** Presence Rate, Present Today, Late Today, Leave Pending, Peak Hours, Coverage.

**Schedule notifications**: new leave requests, approved leaves, with date/time and a **View** button.

### Calendar view

<Frame caption="Monthly calendar: scheduled services per day with Hostess / Baggage Handler color coding and status (Present, Late)">
  ![Planning calendar](/images/guide/admin/planning-calendar.png)
</Frame>

Weekly/monthly grid showing, day by day, the service slots assigned to each staff member (hostesses, porters).

Available tabs: **Calendar**, **Timeline**, **Teams & Rotations**, **Analytics**, **Holidays & Forecasts**, **Notifications**.

### Timeline view

<Frame caption="Timeline: services of the day (morning / afternoon / evening) with Present / Late status, Leaves & Absences section (Approved / Pending / Rejected)">
  ![Planning timeline](/images/guide/admin/planning-timeline.png)
</Frame>

Chronological view of the selected day with the list of leaves and absences to process (bottom panel).

### Teams and rotations

<Frame caption="Teams & Rotations: active rotations (Team A Week 1), staff preferences, auto generation in Balanced / Peak modes">
  ![Teams and rotations](/images/guide/admin/planning-teams-rotations.png)
</Frame>

- **Active Rotations**: team composition per week.
- **Staff Preferences**: individual time preferences.
- **Auto Generation**: automatic schedule generation with two modes:
  - **Balanced**: even distribution across staff.
  - **Peak**: priority coverage of peak hours.

### Planning analytics

<Frame caption="Analytics: 30-day presence rate, Top Performers, per-agent detail (present / late / absent)">
  ![Planning analytics](/images/guide/admin/planning-analytics.png)
</Frame>

Track planning performance: 30-day presence rate, top performers ranking, per-agent punctuality detail.

### Holidays and forecasts

<Frame caption="Holidays & Forecasts: total 23 holidays, table of recurring days with dates and multipliers">
  ![Holidays and forecasts](/images/guide/admin/planning-holidays.png)
</Frame>

List of holidays and special events impacting the schedule, with the option to add a new date and a load multiplier.

### Planning notifications

<Frame caption="Planning notifications: approved, rejected or pending leave requests with 'Mark all as read' button">
  ![Planning notifications](/images/guide/admin/planning-notifications.png)
</Frame>

Full history of all notifications generated by the planning module (requests, approvals, rejections).
