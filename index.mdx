## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Your License">
    Choose a license type (Free, Trial, Basic, Premium, or Enterprise) and submit your request at [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com). You'll receive your license key by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In & Configure">
    Log in at [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php) using your credentials, Google account, or phone number. Then set up your agency profile, add routes, and configure your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Managing">
    Create your first trip, assign crew, open reservations, and issue your first QR code ticket — all from the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  GoTravelXpress is fully compatible with Mobile Money services popular across Africa — including MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, and Wave — as well as Visa/Mastercard and cash payments.
</Note>
