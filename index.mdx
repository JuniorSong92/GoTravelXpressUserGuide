## Get started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request your license">
    On [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php), pick a license type (Free Trial or Pro Plan) and submit your request. You receive the key by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and activate">
    Sign in on [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php) with your Administrator credentials. In **Settings > License**, paste the key to activate your platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and sell">
    Add agencies, routes, vehicles, and create Operator, Baggage Handler, and Hostess accounts. Open your first trip and issue your first QR ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  GoTravelXpress is fully compatible with the Mobile Money services popular across Africa — MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Wave — plus Visa/Mastercard and cash payments.
</Note>
