GoTravelXpress runs on a **perpetual license model**: a one-time purchase, no subscription, no hidden fees. Once activated in the Administrator space, the modules covered by your plan are unlocked for life on your installation. Five tiers cover the needs of a single agency to a multi-site operation.

## Tier overview

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="gift">
    Basic access to the main interface to discover the platform, with no commitment. Limited features.
  </Card>

  <Card title="14-day Trial" icon="clock">
    All features unlocked for 14 days: API, analytics, and multi-agency included. Ideal for real-world testing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Basic" icon="building">
    Essential modules: reservations, QR tickets, payments, and fleet management. Perfect for a single agency starting digital.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Premium" icon="star">
    Every module, including the REST API, advanced analytics, and the full baggage and crew suite.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="buildings">
    Premium + multi-agency management with per-branch roles and permissions, white-label, and priority support.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Use the **Free** license for a quick tour, or the **14-day Trial** to validate every module (QR tickets, Mobile Money, REST API) before buying a paid plan.
</Info>

## Request a license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the discovery page">
    Go to [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php) and scroll to **Request a License**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Provide:

    <ParamField body="License Type" type="enum" required>
      `Free`, `14-day Trial`, `Basic`, `Premium`, or `Enterprise`.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your Name" type="string" required>
      License holder's name.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your Email" type="string" required>
      Address where the key will be sent. Double-check it.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your Company" type="string">
      Transport agency name.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Specific Requirements" type="string">
      Multi-agency, API integration, white-label, operator count.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and receive the key">
    Click **Submit Request**. The GoTravelXpress team validates your request and sends the key by email. Check your spam folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate the license in the Administrator space

<Warning>
  Key activation happens **only in the Administrator space** (the first account you create). No other role (Operator, Baggage Handler, Hostess) sees this menu.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in as Administrator">
    Open [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php) and sign in with your Administrator credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings > License">
    In the Admin side menu, click **Settings**, then the **License** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key and activate">
    Paste the key you received by email into the **Activation key** field and click **Activate**. The type, expiry date, and unlocked modules show up immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each key is bound to **one installation** and to the Administrator agency that activates it. To migrate or upgrade, email [contact@prozigzag.com](mailto:contact@prozigzag.com).
</Note>

## Check your active license

License status is visible in **Settings > License** of the Administrator space: type, activation date, expiry date (if any), and the list of unlocked modules.
