GoTravelXpress operates on a **perpetual license model** — you make a single purchase and your access never expires. There are no monthly subscriptions, no annual renewal fees, and no hidden charges. Once you activate your license key, every module covered by your plan is yours to use indefinitely. GoTravelXpress offers five license tiers designed to fit agencies at every stage, from a first-time operator evaluating the platform to a large multi-branch transport company requiring white-label branding and priority support.

## License Tiers at a Glance

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="gift">
    **Best for:** Agencies that want to explore the platform with no commitment.

    The Free license gives you basic access to the core interface so you can get familiar with GoTravelXpress before deciding on a paid plan. Feature availability is limited, but it is a no-risk way to see the platform in action.
  </Card>
  <Card title="14-Day Trial" icon="clock">
    **Best for:** Agencies ready to evaluate the full platform before purchasing.

    The Trial license unlocks every feature and every module for 14 days — including the SaaS API, analytics, and multi-agency tools. This is the recommended starting point for any agency that wants to run real-world tests before committing to a license.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Basic" icon="building">
    **Best for:** Small agencies getting started with digital operations.

    The Basic license activates the core modules your day-to-day operations depend on: reservations, QR ticket issuance, payments, and fleet management. It gives you a solid digital foundation without paying for advanced features you may not need yet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premium" icon="star">
    **Best for:** Growing agencies that need the full feature set including API access.

    The Premium license unlocks all modules available in GoTravelXpress, including the fully documented REST API for integrating with external systems, advanced analytics dashboards, and the complete crew and baggage management suite.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="buildings">
    **Best for:** Large transport groups, multi-agency networks, and white-label deployments.

    The Enterprise license includes everything in Premium, plus multi-agency management with independent role and permission controls per branch, white-label branding options, and priority support from the GoTravelXpress team.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  The **Free** and **14-day Trial** licenses are ideal for evaluation. Use the Free license for a no-commitment introduction, or request the 14-day Trial to test every feature end to end — including QR tickets, Mobile Money payments, and the REST API — before choosing a paid tier.
</Info>

## How to Request a License

Requesting a license takes less than two minutes. The GoTravelXpress team reviews your request and sends your license key by email.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Visit the GoTravelXpress Website">
    Go to [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com) and scroll to the **Request a License** section at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill Out the License Request Form">
    Complete the form with your details:

    <ParamField body="Your Name" type="string" required>
      Your full name or the name of the person responsible for the license.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your Email" type="string" required>
      The email address where you want to receive your license key. Double-check this field — your key will be sent here.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your Company" type="string">
      Your transport agency or business name.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Specific Requirements" type="string">
      Describe any special needs for your deployment — for example, multi-agency setup, API integration, white-label branding, or a specific number of operators.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Message" type="string">
      An optional free-text message to the GoTravelXpress team with any additional context or questions.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="License Type" type="enum" required>
      Select one of: `Free`, `14-day Trial`, `Basic`, `Premium`, or `Enterprise`.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit Your Request">
    Click **Submit Request**. A confirmation message will appear on screen to confirm that your request has been received. The GoTravelXpress team validates requests and issues license keys by email, typically within a short turnaround.

    <Note>
      Check your spam or junk mail folder if you do not see the email arrive. The license key is sent from the GoTravelXpress team at Eagle Tech.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive Your License Key by Email">
    Once your request is validated, you will receive an email containing your unique license key. Keep this key safe — it is tied to your installation and cannot be transferred.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate Your License">
    After signing in to GoTravelXpress for the first time at [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php), you will be prompted to enter your license key. Paste the key from your email and confirm. Your chosen plan activates immediately, unlocking all the modules covered by your tier.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## License Activation

When you sign in for the first time, GoTravelXpress checks whether a valid license is associated with your installation. If no license has been activated yet, you will see a prompt to enter your license key. Paste the key you received by email, click **Activate**, and the platform unlocks the modules covered by your plan.

<Note>
  Each license key is valid for **one installation**. If you need to migrate your installation or upgrade your license tier, contact the support team at **contact@prozigzag.com** — upgrades are available at any time by reaching out directly.
</Note>

If you ever need to check which license is currently active on your installation, you can find this information in the **Agency Management** or **Settings** section of your dashboard.
