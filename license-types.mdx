GoTravelXpress ships with **two license types only**: **Free Trial** to evaluate the platform and **Pro Plan** for full perpetual access. Activation happens exclusively in the Administrator space.

## The two licenses

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free Trial" icon="clock">
    Trial license to evaluate the platform. Temporary access to every module with usage limits. Ideal to test GoTravelXpress before you buy.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pro Plan" icon="star">
    Perpetual license with full access to every module: reservations, QR tickets, Mobile Money payments, fleet, baggage, analytics, multi-agency, and REST API. One-time purchase, no subscription.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Start with the **Free Trial** to explore the platform in real conditions, then upgrade to **Pro Plan** when you are ready to deploy without limits.
</Info>

## Request a license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the landing page">
    Visit [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php) and scroll to **Request a License**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Provide:

    <ParamField body="License type" type="enum" required>
      `Free Trial` or `Pro Plan`.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your name" type="string" required>
      The license owner's name.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your email" type="string" required>
      The address where the key will be sent. Check it carefully.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Your company" type="string">
      The transport agency name.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Specific needs" type="string">
      Multi-agency, API integration, number of operators, particular constraints.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and receive the key">
    Click **Submit request**. The GoTravelXpress team validates your request and sends the key by email. Remember to check your spam folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate the license as Administrator

<Warning>
  Key activation happens **exclusively in the Administrator space** (the first account to create). No other role (Operator, Baggage Handler, Hostess) can see this menu.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in as Administrator">
    Open [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php) and sign in with your Administrator credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings > License">
    In the Admin side menu, click **Settings**, then the **License** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key and activate">
    Paste the key you received by email into the **Activation key** field and click **Activate**. The license type, expiry date (if any), and unlocked modules appear immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each key is tied to **one installation** and to the Administrator agency that activates it. To migrate your installation or move from Free Trial to Pro Plan, email [juniorsong92@icloud.com](mailto:juniorsong92@icloud.com).
</Note>

## Move from Free Trial to Pro Plan

When your trial nears its end, your data is kept. Simply request a **Pro Plan** key from the form, then paste it in the same place (**Settings > License**). Your agencies, routes, users, and history stay intact.

## Check your active license

The license status is shown in **Settings > License** in the Administrator space: type (Free Trial or Pro Plan), activation date, expiry date (for Free Trial), and the list of unlocked modules.
