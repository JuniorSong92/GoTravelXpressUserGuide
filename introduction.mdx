GoTravelXpress is the SaaS platform designed by Eagle Tech to digitize transport and travel agencies. It bundles **8 integrated modules** and **4 user roles** into one cloud dashboard, under a **perpetual license**: no subscription, no hidden fees.

## Who is it for?

- Bus and intercity coach companies
- Ground travel agencies
- Multi-agency networks across Africa and beyond
- Parcel transport operators

## The 4 user roles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Administrator / Manager" icon="user-shield" href="/agency/user-roles">
    Activates the license, manages agencies, routes, pricing, users, and sees all statistics.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Agency Operator" icon="ticket" href="/agency/user-roles">
    Sells tickets at the counter, collects Mobile Money, card, or cash, and prints QR tickets.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Baggage Handler" icon="suitcase-rolling" href="/agency/user-roles">
    Registers bags and parcels, prints labels, scans QR codes at boarding.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hostess / Crew Chief" icon="user-check" href="/agency/user-roles">
    Scans QR tickets on board, clocks in crew with geolocated QR, reports incidents.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
