GoTravelXpress is an all-in-one cloud platform built specifically for transport and travel agencies that want to ditch paper-based operations and manage their entire business from a single, modern dashboard. Whether you run a single shuttle service or a multi-branch intercity bus network, GoTravelXpress brings together reservations, QR code ticketing, payments, fleet management, crew scheduling, baggage tracking, and real-time analytics under one roof — all unlocked with a single perpetual license. You buy it once, and you keep every module, every feature, and every future update, with no recurring subscription fees.

## Supported Regions & Languages

GoTravelXpress is built with African transport markets in mind. It natively supports **Mobile Money** payment providers — including MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, and Wave — which are the dominant payment channels across Central, West, and East Africa. The platform is equally suited to agencies processing Visa/Mastercard and cash payments anywhere in the world.

The interface is fully available in **9 languages**:

| Language | Language | Language |
|---|---|---|
| 🇬🇧 English | 🇫🇷 Français | 🇪🇸 Español |
| 🇸🇦 العربية | 🇮🇹 Italiano | 🇵🇹 Português |
| 🇷🇺 Русский | 🇩🇪 Deutsch | 🇨🇳 中文 |

You can switch the interface language at any time from the login screen or from within your account settings.

## System Requirements

GoTravelXpress is fully cloud-based — there is nothing to download or install on your computer. All you need to get started is:

- A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari)
- A stable internet connection

Your data is stored securely in the cloud, so you can access your agency dashboard from any device, from any location, at any time.
