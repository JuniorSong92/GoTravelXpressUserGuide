GoTravelXpress enables Agency Operators to manage the full reservation lifecycle: from selecting a seat on the interactive seat map to issuing the ticket and processing its refund. This page covers the in-person sales steps and the modification/cancellation rules set by the Administrator.

## Ticket statuses

A ticket in GoTravelXpress moves through several statuses based on its payment and usage:

- **Reserved**: seat temporarily held without payment.
- **Paid**: ticket confirmed and paid by the customer or the operator.
- **Boarded**: passenger validated at boarding (GPS check-in or QR scan).
- **Canceled**: reservation canceled according to the current policy.
- **Refunded**: canceled ticket with a refund issued through the Payments module.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for a trip">
    From the counter, enter the departure city, destination, and travel date to display available routes in GoTravelXpress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a seat">
    The interactive seat map displays each seat as free, held, or sold. Select a free seat for the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter passenger details">
    Fill in the passenger information: first name, last name, phone, ID card, and category if applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay and issue the ticket">
    Process payment at the counter or record an online reservation for deferred payment. The ticket is then generated with its boarding QR code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Online booking by the customer

The customer can book online from the search page (welcome.php) without operator intervention. After selecting the trip and seat:

- a payment deadline is granted (configurable by the Administrator in Settings) ;
- if payment is not made within this period, the seat automatically reverts to **free** ;
- the operator can monitor these pending reservations and finalize payment at the counter.

## Interactive seat map

The seat map is the central tool for reservations. It allows you to:

- display the physical layout of the vehicle (bus, minibus, coach) ;
- visually identify seats and their status ;
- temporarily block a seat during the sales process.

### Visual statuses

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Seat available for reservation. |
| **Held** | Reservation in progress or payment pending. |
| **Sold** | Ticket already paid and confirmed. |

## Modifications and cancellations

Modification and cancellation rules are defined by the Administrator through the license and settings module:

- **Modification**: change of date, trip, or seat, subject to permissions and availability.
- **Cancellation**: generates a canceled ticket and, if configured, initiates an automatic refund to be processed through the [Payments](/modules/payments) module.
- **Penalty**: the withholding amount and deadlines are set by the Administrator.
