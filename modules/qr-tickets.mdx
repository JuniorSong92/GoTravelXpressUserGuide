GoTravelXpress automatically generates a unique encrypted QR code for every ticket issued. This ticket can be printed at the counter by the Agency Operator, or sent directly to the customer by email, SMS, or WhatsApp. The Hostess scans the QR at boarding to validate access, while the Baggage Handler uses it to associate luggage and parcels with the trip. Each QR token is single-use and can only be reissued by an Administrator.

## Issuing a QR Ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Issue the ticket">
    At the counter sale, the Agency Operator finalizes the booking. GoTravelXpress then automatically generates the unique encrypted QR code associated with that ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver to customer">
    The Agency Operator can print the ticket with its QR code, or send the digital ticket to the customer by email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    The ticket status appears in the system as "issued" and ready for validation at boarding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Boarding Workflow

1. The customer presents their ticket (paper or digital) to the Hostess
2. The Hostess scans the QR with their terminal
3. GoTravelXpress verifies the token's authenticity and uniqueness
4. The ticket status switches to "validated" with the location and time logged
5. The customer is authorized to board

<Warning>
  Once scanned, the QR token is immediately invalidated and can no longer be reused. Any rescan attempt will show an already-used ticket alert. In case of a technical issue during scanning, contact an Administrator immediately to proceed with a reissue from the license management panel.
</Warning>

## Luggage and Parcel Tracking

The Baggage Handler uses the same ticket QR code to register and track the luggage and parcels associated with the journey:

- Scan the ticket QR to associate each bag with the passenger
- Record the number of parcels and weight
- Verify matching during loading and delivery
- Full traceability from traveler to personal belongings
