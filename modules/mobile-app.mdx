The GoTravelXpress mobile app extends the web platform to smartphones. It is available for **drivers**, **end customers**, and **agency agents** (Operator, Baggage Handler, Hostess), with role-specific interfaces. Single sign-on takes each profile straight to its own workspace after login.

## Driver features

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    The driver opens the app and enters credentials or activates Face Login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View today's trips">
    The home screen lists every assigned departure with time, route, and vehicle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock in">
    The driver scans the geolocated start-of-service QR. GPS position is recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the mission">
    The app shows boarded passengers, stops, and any conflict alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock out">
    On arrival, the driver scans the end-of-service QR to close the mission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Agency agent features

The app lets an **Agency Operator** sell tickets from a phone or tablet:

- Search trip and pick seat on the interactive map
- Collect Mobile Money (MTN, Orange, Wave), card, or cash
- Instant QR ticket generation
- Automatic send via SMS or WhatsApp to the customer
- Sales history for the workstation

For the **Baggage Handler**:
- Scan the passenger QR ticket to attach a bag
- Record weight and description
- Print a barcode label to a Bluetooth printer
- Mark items Loaded / Delivered / Unclaimed

For the **Hostess / Crew Chief**:
- Scan QR tickets at boarding (status Boarded / Absent / Late)
- Geolocated clock-in for own presence
- Report an incident from a dedicated form

## Security and connectivity

<Info>
The app communicates with the GoTravelXpress server over TLS. Some actions (QR scan, trip lookup) work in degraded offline mode and sync as soon as the connection returns.
</Info>

<Warning>
Geolocation must be authorized for driver, baggage handler, and hostess clock-ins to be valid. Without permission, the clock-in QR is rejected server-side.
</Warning>
