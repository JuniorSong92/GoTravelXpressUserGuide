This quickstart takes you from license request to your first QR ticket: request a key on the public landing page, activate it in the Admin space, create Operator, Baggage Handler, and Hostess accounts, then open your first trip.

## Role overview

GoTravelXpress is a multi-role system. Each profile has a dedicated dashboard with strict permissions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Administrator / Manager" icon="user-shield">
    Activates the license, creates agencies, manages users, routes, pricing, and sees all statistics.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Agency Operator" icon="ticket">
    Opens counter ticket sales, collects payments, and prints QR tickets for customers.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Baggage Handler" icon="suitcase-rolling">
    Registers bags and parcels, scans QR codes at boarding and delivery.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hostess / Crew Chief" icon="user-check">
    Scans QR tickets on board, validates attendance, and clocks in crew with geolocation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Step 1: Request your license

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open the discovery page">
    Go to [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php) and click **Get my license**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a license type">
    Select **Free Trial** or **Pro Plan**. See [License Types](/license-types) to compare.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the request">
    Fill in your name, email, company, and specific requirements, then submit. You will receive your license key by email after validation.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Step 2: Activate the license in the Admin space

License activation happens **only in the Administrator space**. This is the first account to create.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php) and sign in with your Administrator credentials (email + password, Google, or phone).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings > License">
    In the Admin dashboard, open the **Settings** menu, then **License**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the license key">
    Paste the key you received by email into the **Activation key** field and click **Activate**. The license type, expiry date, and unlocked modules appear immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The license is bound to the Administrator agency that activated it. Every agency, user, or trip you create inherits its limits (agency count, operators, vehicles, etc.).
</Note>

## Step 3: Configure your agency

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your main agency">
    **Agencies > New agency**: name, city, country, logo, contact details, currency, and time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add routes and vehicles">
    **Fleet** menu: add your buses (license plate, capacity, category). **Routes** menu: define origin / destination pairs and pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create accounts for operational roles">
    **Users > New**: create an account for each **Agency Operator**, **Baggage Handler**, and **Hostess**. Assign the matching role; permissions are set automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Create your first trip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan the trip">
    **Planning** menu: pick a route, date, departure time, vehicle, and assign driver + hostess. The seat map is generated automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open sales">
    Click **Publish**. Operators can start selling tickets at the counter and customers can book online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue a QR ticket">
    In the **Operator** space, pick the trip, choose an available seat, record the customer, collect payment (Mobile Money, card, cash): the ticket with QR code is generated and printable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register baggage">
    The **Baggage Handler** space scans the ticket, registers bags and parcels, and prints tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Control boarding">
    On board, the **Hostess** scans each passenger's QR to validate access and clocks in their own attendance with a geolocated QR scan.
  </Step>
</Steps>
