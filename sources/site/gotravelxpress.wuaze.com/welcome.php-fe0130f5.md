## FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our platform

Does the SaaS work in Africa?

Yes, our solution is fully compatible with Mobile Money payments (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Wave, etc.) and adapted to the specifics of the African market.

Can I manage multiple agencies?

Absolutely! Our platform supports multi-agency management with customizable user roles and granular permissions for each site.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Mobile Money, Visa/Mastercard bank cards, bank transfers and cash payments with automatic invoice generation.

Are the tickets secure?

Yes, all our digital tickets include a unique and encrypted QR code, verifiable in real time to avoid fraud and duplicates.

Do you offer an API?

Yes, we offer a fully documented REST API to integrate our solution with your other systems (ERP, accounting, mobile apps).
