## What to Include in Your Message

To help the support team resolve your request as quickly as possible, include the following details when you reach out:

- **License key** — your current license key, if you have one
- **Agency name** — the name of your agency or organization
- **Description of the issue** — a clear summary of what you're experiencing or asking about
- **Steps to reproduce** — if reporting a bug or unexpected behavior, describe the exact steps that lead to the issue
- **Screenshots** — attach any relevant screenshots that help illustrate the problem

The more context you provide, the faster the team can diagnose and resolve your request.
