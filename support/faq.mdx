This page collects the most frequently asked questions from transport agencies using GoTravelXpress. It covers internal roles, license activation, key ticketing features, and support and contact options.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Is GoTravelXpress a SaaS built for transport in Africa?">
    Yes. GoTravelXpress is a SaaS platform developed by Eagle Tech specifically for African transport agencies. The software addresses local realities: intermittent connectivity, multi-agency operations, counter workflows, and the need for luggage and parcel traceability.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple agencies with a single account?">
    Yes. GoTravelXpress supports multi-agency management. The administrator can structure multiple counters within the system and assign specific operators to each site. This centralizes sales, reporting, and inventory while maintaining a local logic per agency.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are supported?">
    GoTravelXpress integrates payment methods suited to the African context, including cash collection at the counter, mobile payments (Mobile Money), and bank transfers depending on the integration in place. All collections are tracked and centralized for budget reporting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are issued tickets secure and verifiable?">
    Yes. Every ticket issued by GoTravelXpress carries a secure identifiable code. The hostess can scan a QR code at boarding to validate ticket authenticity, prevent duplication, and ensure full passenger traceability.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does GoTravelXpress provide an API for third-party integrations?">
    Yes. An API is available to connect GoTravelXpress with other systems: online booking platforms, accounting solutions, or external business tools. To obtain documentation or request access, contact our team on the [Support](/support/contact) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is authorized to activate the agency license?">
    Only the **Administrator** can activate the agency license. This activation is done in the **Settings > License** menu. No other role (Operator, Baggage Handler, Hostess) has access to this function.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does each role do in GoTravelXpress?">
    The platform defines four essential operational roles:

    <ul>
  <li><strong>Administrator</strong>: manages the license, global configuration, users, and agency settings.</li>
  <li><strong>Agency Operator</strong>: handles counter sales, ticket issuance, and departure monitoring from the central interface.</li>
  <li><strong>Baggage Handler</strong>: registers luggage and parcels, assigns tags, and oversees freight traceability on board.</li>
  <li><strong>Hostess</strong>: manages boarding via QR code scanning and records the geolocated attendance of each traveler.</li>
</ul>

    For more details, see the [User roles](/agency/user-roles) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my license expires?">
    If the license expires, access to operational features is suspended. The administrator can renew the license in **Settings > License** by contacting commercial support. Read-only access generally remains available to view history, but sales and boarding are blocked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact GoTravelXpress support?">
    Support is reachable by email, phone, and WhatsApp. Our team provides 24/7 assistance for operational emergencies. Find all contact details and topics on the [Contact](/support/contact) page.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
