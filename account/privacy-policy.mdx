This policy explains how GoTravelXpress handles your personal data when you use the platform. It covers everything from how your account activity is treated, to how data is stored on our servers, to the transparency commitments and encryption standards that protect your information. GoTravelXpress is built and maintained by Eagle Tech, based in Yaoundé, Cameroon.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Account Activity & Privacy" icon="user-shield">
    GoTravelXpress does not store detailed logs of user activity in any identifiable form. When you use the platform to manage reservations, tickets, payments, or crew operations, the following commitments apply:

    - **No communication or reservation content retention.** GoTravelXpress does not retain the content of your communications or travel reservation details in a form that can be traced back to you personally.
    - **No discrimination by device, browser, or protocol.** You receive the same quality of service regardless of whether you access GoTravelXpress from a desktop, mobile device, specific browser, or network protocol.
    - **No speed restrictions.** GoTravelXpress does not impose bandwidth or access speed restrictions on your use of the platform, except in cases of excessive or abusive usage that would negatively affect the experience of other users.
    - **Free and trial services included.** Where free or trial access is offered, these same privacy commitments apply — no lesser standard is used for non-paying users.

    <Info>
      These principles mean that your day-to-day platform use — signing in, creating reservations, processing payments, and generating reports — leaves no identifiable trace in detailed server logs.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Server Security & Data Storage" icon="server">
    All data required to operate your account and manage your agency's reservations is hosted on dedicated, secure servers owned and controlled by GoTravelXpress or its subsidiaries.

    - **Encrypted storage.** Every piece of data stored on GoTravelXpress servers is encrypted. Encryption is applied both at rest and in transit so that data is protected at every stage.
    - **Jurisdiction with strong data protection.** GoTravelXpress servers are located in countries that enforce strict data protection legislation, providing legal as well as technical safeguards for your information.
    - **Minimal sensitive data retention.** GoTravelXpress is designed to minimize the amount of sensitive or identifiable data held on servers. In the unlikely event of a security incident, no detailed user logs or sensitive identifiable records would be exposed, because such data is not retained by design.

    <Note>
      GoTravelXpress uses **100% dedicated servers** — your agency data is not co-mingled on shared infrastructure with unrelated third-party tenants.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transparency" icon="eye">
    GoTravelXpress is committed to being open and honest about how it responds to legal or official requests for data disclosure.

    - **Transparency reports.** Any legally mandated requests for user data disclosure — such as those arising from court orders or regulatory investigations — will be summarized and published in a transparency report available on the GoTravelXpress website.
    - **You will be informed where permitted.** Where the law allows GoTravelXpress to notify an affected user about a data request, it will do so.
    - **No voluntary data sharing.** GoTravelXpress does not voluntarily share user data with governments, advertisers, or other third parties outside of what is legally required.

    <Info>
      Transparency reports are published on the GoTravelXpress website as they become relevant. Check back periodically or contact [contact@prozigzag.com](mailto:contact@prozigzag.com) to inquire about the current report.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Data Protection Standards" icon="lock">
    GoTravelXpress applies internationally recognized data protection standards across all aspects of its infrastructure and operations.

    - **256-bit encryption.** All data — including account credentials, reservation records, passenger information, and payment data — is protected with robust 256-bit encryption techniques.
    - **Physical and technical server control.** GoTravelXpress owns and directly operates the majority of its server infrastructure, ensuring both physical access control and technical security management remain in-house.
    - **International standards compliance.** Account data, personal information, and reservation histories are secured according to internationally recognized data protection standards.
    - **GDPR compliance.** GoTravelXpress is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which means you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data where applicable.

    <Tip>
      If you would like to exercise your data rights under GDPR — such as requesting a copy of your data or asking for deletion — contact GoTravelXpress directly using the details below.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
