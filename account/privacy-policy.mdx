This page summarizes the privacy policy for the GoTravelXpress SaaS platform. It covers the four pillars Eagle Tech applies to all data from transport agencies and their passengers: Account Activity, Server Privacy, Transparency, and Data Security.

## 1. Account Activity

GoTravelXpress only collects the information needed to operate your agency: registration details, user preferences, booking history, and license settings. This data is only used to deliver the service. The Agency Administrator controls license activation from Settings > License.

## 2. Server Privacy

The infrastructure runs on high-availability dedicated servers. Information is protected with advanced 256-bit encryption and best-in-class network security. Only authorized Eagle Tech staff can access systems, strictly for professional purposes under audit trails.

## 3. Transparency

Eagle Tech does not keep detailed logs of individual user activities. We do not share, sell, or rent your information to third parties for commercial purposes. Any technical sharing required (hosting, major updates) is governed by strict confidentiality agreements.

## 4. Data Security

Security is our top priority. We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

</Note>
