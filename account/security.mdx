GoTravelXpress, the Eagle Tech SaaS platform for transport agencies, enforces strict security measures to protect passenger data, staff information, and operations. This page explains the technical and organizational safeguards in place, as well as the best practices you should follow.

## Encryption and data in transit

Every exchange between your workstation and the GoTravelXpress platform is secured with TLS. This applies to:

- the sign-in page ([https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/index.php))
- the public landing page ([https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php](https://gotravelxpress.wuaze.com/welcome.php))
- all internal dashboards and forms

Encryption prevents interception of credentials, payment information, and personal passenger data over public or shared networks.

## Authentication and sessions

### Hashed passwords

When a password is created or updated, GoTravelXpress applies irreversible hashing with a unique salt. Even in the event of unauthorized database access, passwords remain protected.

### Session expiry

User sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity. This reduces the risk of fraudulent use on shared or exposed workstations that are left unattended, which is especially important in busy transport agency environments.

### Failure lockout

After several consecutive failed sign-in attempts, the account is temporarily locked. The lockout duration increases with repeated attempts. This protection applies to all authentication methods, including Face Login.

## Biometric Face Login

<Card title="Tip for counter workstations" icon="lightbulb" horizontal>
  Face Login is especially useful for Agency Operators working at the counter. It speeds up authentication while reducing the risk of password sharing on workstations accessible to the public.
</Card>

Facial recognition is offered for exposed workstations (counter, boarding). Facial features are captured, transformed into a secure digital template, and compared during each authentication. This method is optional and can be disabled by the Administrator in the agency settings.

## Administrator audit logs

The Administrator of a GoTravelXpress agency can review audit logs from the management console. These logs include:

- sign-in dates and times per user
- critical configuration changes (licenses, agency settings)
- actions on sensitive data (ticket validation, boarding check-in, baggage handling)
- account lockout alerts

These traces help detect abnormal activity and meet the traceability needs of transport agencies.

## Security best practices

Follow these recommendations to strengthen your agency's security:

- **Strong password**: use at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Do not reuse a password from another service.
- **Do not share credentials**: every GoTravelXpress user should have their own account, even at the counter.
- **Lock your workstation**: lock your session or sign out when leaving an exposed workstation.
- **Revoke ex-employee access**: the Administrator should immediately disable accounts for staff who leave the agency.
- **Review audit logs regularly**: check logs periodically to spot any unusual sign-in activity.
