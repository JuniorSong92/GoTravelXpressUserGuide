GoTravelXpress supports multiple authentication methods so every team member can access the platform securely. Whether you prefer a classic username and password, a quick Google sign-in, an OTP sent to your phone, or biometric Face Login, this page walks you through each method and helps you troubleshoot common access issues.

## Login Methods

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Username & Password">
    The standard sign-in method works with your registered username, phone number, or email address.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the login page">
        Navigate to your GoTravelXpress instance URL and click **Sign In**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your credentials">
        Type your **username**, **phone number**, or **email address** in the first field, then enter your **password** in the second field.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Click the **Sign In** button. On success, you are redirected to your agency dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If you manage multiple agencies, make sure you are entering the credentials for the correct agency account before signing in.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Google Sign-In">
    Link your Google account for a one-click sign-in experience without entering a password each time.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Sign in with Google">
        On the login page, click the **Sign in with Google** button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize GoTravelXpress">
        A Google authorization window opens. Select your Google account and click **Allow** to grant GoTravelXpress access.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Access your dashboard">
        After successful authorization, you are automatically redirected to your GoTravelXpress dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Google Sign-In requires that your Google account email matches the email address registered on your GoTravelXpress account. Contact your administrator if the emails do not match.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Phone Login">
    Phone Login sends a one-time password (OTP) to your registered phone number so you can sign in without a password.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Phone Login">
        On the login page, click **Phone Login**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your phone number">
        Type your registered phone number, including the country code (e.g., `+237 6XX XXX XXX`).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Receive your OTP">
        GoTravelXpress sends a one-time password via SMS to the number you entered. The code is valid for a short window — use it promptly.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify and access">
        Enter the OTP in the verification field and click **Verify**. You are then taken to your dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If you do not receive the OTP within 60 seconds, check that your phone number is correct and tap **Resend Code**.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Face Login">
    Face Login uses your device camera for biometric authentication, giving you hands-free, password-free access.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Face Login">
        On the login page, click **Face Login**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Allow camera access">
        Your browser will prompt you to allow camera access. Click **Allow** to proceed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Position your face">
        Ensure you are in a well-lit environment with your face clearly visible to the camera. Avoid strong backlighting or shadows across your face.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete the face scan">
        Hold your device steady and look directly at the camera. GoTravelXpress completes the biometric scan and, once your identity is confirmed, grants access to your dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Face Login privacy: biometric data is processed locally on your device and is **not** transmitted to or stored on GoTravelXpress servers. Your facial data never leaves your browser session.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Language Selection

The GoTravelXpress login page includes a language selector so you can use the platform in your preferred language before you even sign in.

GoTravelXpress currently supports the following languages:

| Language   | Language   |
|------------|------------|
| English    | Arabic     |
| French     | Italian    |
| Spanish    | Portuguese |
| Russian    | German     |
| Chinese    |            |

To switch languages, locate the language selector on the login page and choose your preferred option from the dropdown. Your selection applies immediately to the entire interface session.

<Info>
  Your language preference is saved to your account after you sign in. The next time you log in, the interface loads in the language you last selected.
</Info>

## Forgot Password

If you cannot remember your password, you can reset it through a verified email link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot Password">
    On the login page, click the **Forgot Password** link below the sign-in form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address associated with your GoTravelXpress account and click **Send Reset Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Open the password reset email from GoTravelXpress. If you do not see it within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new password">
    Click the reset link in the email. You are taken to a secure page where you can set a new password. Choose a strong, unique password that you have not used before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your new password">
    Return to the login page and sign in using your email address and the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Reset links expire after a limited time for security reasons. If your link has expired, repeat the process to request a new one.
</Tip>

## Account Lockout

GoTravelXpress temporarily locks your account after a number of consecutive failed sign-in attempts to protect against unauthorized access.

**What happens during a lockout:**
- You will see a message on the login screen indicating that your account has been temporarily locked.
- You cannot attempt to sign in again until the lockout period has passed.
- A notification email may be sent to your registered address to alert you of the failed attempts.

**How to unlock your account:**
- Wait for the lockout period to expire, then try signing in again with the correct credentials.
- If you have forgotten your password, use the [Forgot Password](#forgot-password) flow described above to reset it.
- If you believe your account was locked due to unauthorized access attempts, contact your agency administrator or reach out to GoTravelXpress support at [contact@prozigzag.com](mailto:contact@prozigzag.com) or **+237 695 602 293**.

<Warning>
  Keep your credentials secure and do not share your account with colleagues. Each team member should have their own GoTravelXpress user account with appropriate role-based permissions. Sharing login details removes the audit trail and puts your agency data at risk.
</Warning>
