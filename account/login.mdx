## Available sign-in methods

GoTravelXpress provides several sign-in methods to match different user profiles and workstation constraints.provides several sign-in methods to match different user profiles and workstation constraints.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify your user profile">
    Determine whether you are an Administrator, Agency Operator (counter), Baggage Handler, or Hostess (boarding). Your profile determines the features available after sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Select the method that fits your situation: password-based, Google, phone, Facebook, or biometric Face Login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the requested information">
    Fill in the identifier field (email, phone, or username) and password, or follow the authentication flow of the chosen provider (OTP, OAuth, facial recognition).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your dashboard">
    After successful authentication, you are redirected to the GoTravelXpress workspace that matches your role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Sign in with identifier and password

This is the standard method for most users. Three identifier types are accepted:

- **Email**: the registered email address in GoTravelXpress
- **Phone**: the phone number linked to the account, in local or international format
- **Username**: the unique identifier created during registration

### Sign in with Google

Click the **Continue with Google** button. A popup prompts you to select an authorized Google account. After consent validation, GoTravelXpress retrieves your email and signs you in automatically if it matches an existing account.

### Sign in with phone (OTP)Sign in with phone (OTP)

Click the **Sign in with phone** button. Enter your phone number. A one-time OTP code is sent to you by SMS. Enter this code in the interface to complete the sign-in. ### Sign in with Facebook

Click the **Continue with Facebook** button. Authorize GoTravelXpress to access your public Facebook email. If the email matches an existing account, you are signed in automatically.

### Face Login (facial recognition)

This method is reserved for **exposed workstations**, especially counter terminals and boarding posts.

<Card title="Face Login for exposed workstations" icon="camera" horizontal>
  Enable the workstation camera. Position your face in front of the lens. The biometric system compares your face to the facial template stored in your user profile. Authentication is immediate with no manual entry.
</Card>

Roles concerned primarily include **Agency Operators** (counter) and **Hostesses** (boarding). See [/account/security](/account/security) for more on biometric security.

## Forgot your password?

If you have forgotten your password, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password">
    From the sign-in page, click the **Forgot password?** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address associated with your GoTravelXpress account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    A reset email is sent to that address. Open it and click the secure link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Choose a new password that meets the security requirements, then confirm it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

> If you do not receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

## Security and lockout

GoTravelXpress applies automatic security rules to protect accounts against unauthorized access.

- **Lockout after failures**: after a defined number of consecutive failed sign-in attempts, the account is temporarily locked.
- **Unlock delay**: the lockout duration increases progressively with repeated attempts.
- **Notification**: the agency administrator and the affected user may be alerted when a lockout occurs.

## Available languages

The GoTravelXpress sign-in page and interface are available in multiple languages. You can change the language directly from the sign-in page using the language selector, so multilingual users can operate comfortably in their preferred language.
